# Competitors and research record

Research date: 15 October 2026. Public official product/support documentation reviewed; this was not a hands-on speed test or a complete audit of every current app screen. Vendor performance/privacy statements are their claims, not independently verified findings in this pack. Screens and features can change; review installed apps with lawful test accounts during M02 before asserting visual superiority.

| Competitor | Evidence-backed benchmark | Yashi response (design proposal, not observed superiority) |
|---|---|---|
| Proton VPN | Promotes a free Android offering with no ads and no speed limits; separate free-plan documentation describes no data limits | Be transparent that our proposed free tier is 5 GiB, not unlimited. Compete on clarity/localized onboarding, not a false better-free claim |
| NordVPN | Android guide covers quick connection, server selection and settings | One dominant action, explicit readiness/verification, no map-first complexity required |
| Surfshark | Android home documentation covers connection controls and access to other security products; Bypasser supports split tunneling | Keep a VPN-only home without unrelated tools; split tunneling is a future scope |

Competitive position: “clear, calm Android VPN with understandable limits and connection state.” Do not market network size, speed, privacy assurance or reliability as better than these providers without independent comparative evidence. Visual polish alone does not outperform a mature network.
