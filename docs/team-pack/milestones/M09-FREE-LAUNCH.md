# M09 — Beta, Play submission and controlled free launch

Owner: product lead + release engineer + QA. Depends: M08 signed gate. Estimated effort: 7–14 working days plus external verification/review time.

## Goal

Release a stable, honest free product with spending controls, support and rollback ready.

## Tasks in order

1. Recheck current Play Organization verification, target SDK, VPN/foreground service declarations, Data safety, privacy and deletion obligations. Complete any account-specific testing requirements actually shown in Console; do not assume personal-account rules apply to an organization.
2. Export approved original icon/screenshots/feature graphics using current store requirements. Store text describes VpnService use, actual free limit, supported locations and data handling without false anonymity/speed/unlimited claims.
3. Prepare owner-controlled Play App Signing/recovery process, signed AAB, versioning, reviewer access/test account and required declaration/video evidence. No provider keys/debug settings in build.
4. Start internal/closed beta with controlled enrollment (proposed 100–300 testers, or lower if M08 capacity requires). Collect consented feedback/redacted diagnostics; run five-person usability retest.
5. Operate beta for a proposed 7–14 days, longer if sample/bugs insufficient. Measure actual traffic/user, concurrency, successful connections, crash reports and support burden. Re-estimate costs and free allowance transparently before public promises.
6. Submit review, respond truthfully, fix issues; no fake reviewer bypass modes. Publish real legal/status/support pages and Play link only when ready.
7. Stage rollout in small percentages with owner go/no-go and budget/capacity gates. Maintain ability to halt enrollments, drain nodes, publish incidents and roll back to a known-good compatible build/backend.

## Acceptance tests / gate

App/account policy requirements satisfied and Play release actually approved before calling it public launch. M08 blockers remain closed. Real support and deletion channels work. Beta data meets agreed SLOs with sample/exclusions disclosed. Monthly budget/capacity has approved headroom; notifications do not masquerade as provider hard caps. No payments/ads live. Customer knows exact free allowance and reset before first use.

## Handoff evidence

Play submission/approval state, released build/tag, live routes/listing, beta metrics with sample size, estimated recurring bill, runbooks/on-call contacts, rollback artifact and owner launch approval. Do not force existing free users to pay without separate transparent policy/product review.
